---
title: "When Do My Message Limits Reset"
category: usage
---
Message limits reset on a rolling 24-hour window measured from your first message after the previous reset — not at midnight, and not on a fixed daily schedule. If your first message today was at 9:14 AM, your limit resets at 9:14 AM tomorrow.

Team plan pooled usage resets on the same rolling-window basis, tracked from the org's first message in the current window rather than per-seat.

Unused messages do not roll over or accumulate — hitting 0 remaining and waiting for reset is the only way to regain access before upgrading.
