---
title: "Choosing Between Team and Enterprise"
category: plans
---
Team fits small-to-mid-size groups (3–200 seats) that want shared billing and a basic admin console but don't require enterprise identity infrastructure.

Move to Enterprise when you need any of the following, none of which are available on Team:
- SSO/SAML login through your corporate identity provider.
- A custom data retention policy (Team uses the standard 30-day retention for deleted content).
- Invoice-based billing instead of credit card.
- A dedicated support contact with contractual response-time guarantees.
- Custom rate limits above Team's pooled usage caps.

Enterprise pricing and rate limits are negotiated per contract based on seat count and expected usage — contact sales rather than self-serve upgrading for this tier.
