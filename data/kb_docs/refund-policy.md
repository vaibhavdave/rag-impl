---
title: "Refund Policy"
category: billing
---
Nimbus AI offers refunds in these cases:
- **Duplicate charge**: a technical error caused you to be billed twice in one cycle — fully refundable, no time limit.
- **First-time subscribers**: full refund if requested within 7 days of your first charge, no questions asked.
- **Service outage**: if a documented Nimbus AI outage prevented you from using the service for more than 24 continuous hours during a billing cycle, you can request a prorated credit for the affected period.

Refunds are **not** issued for: normal cancellations after the 7-day window, downgrades mid-cycle, or dissatisfaction with model output quality.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms in their signed agreement rather than this policy.
