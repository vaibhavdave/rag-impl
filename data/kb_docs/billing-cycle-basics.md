---
title: "How Billing Cycles Work"
category: billing
---
Subscription billing runs on a monthly cycle anchored to the day you first subscribed. For example, if you subscribed on the 14th, you're charged on the 14th of every following month.

Team and Enterprise plans on invoice billing instead run on a monthly or quarterly cycle set in the contract, with payment due net-30 from invoice date.

Charges appear on your statement as "NIMBUS AI SUBSCRIPTION." If you add seats mid-cycle on a Team plan, the added seats are prorated for the remainder of the current cycle and billed immediately; they're then included in the normal renewal amount going forward.

You can view your next charge date and amount at any time from Account Settings → Billing → Overview.
