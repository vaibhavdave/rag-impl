---
title: "I Was Charged Twice"
category: billing
---
Duplicate charges are almost always one of two things:

1. **Two separate subscriptions** — most often this happens when a Free account was upgraded to Pro, and separately an old/second account under a different email was also subscribed. Check Account Settings → Billing for the subscription ID tied to each charge.
2. **A genuine billing error** — a retry from a previously failed payment succeeding alongside the original charge going through late. This is fully refundable under the Refund Policy.

If you see two charges of the identical amount on the same statement date, it's very likely case 2 — a refund can be processed immediately. If the charges are for different accounts/emails, support will need to confirm which account you intend to keep before merging or cancelling the duplicate.
