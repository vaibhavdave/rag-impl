---
title: "Upgrading and Downgrading Your Plan"
category: plans
---
**Upgrading** (e.g., Free → Pro, Pro → Team) takes effect immediately. You're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle at the new plan's rate, and your usage limits increase right away.

**Downgrading** (e.g., Team → Pro, Pro → Free) takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle, not immediately. You keep your current plan's features and limits until the cycle ends, and you will not receive a partial refund for the difference. This avoids losing access to in-progress work (like large file uploads or long conversations) mid-cycle.

If you downgrade from Team to Pro, any seats beyond the first are removed at the cycle boundary, and affected teammates are notified by email one week in advance. Shared workspace conversations become read-only for removed seats but are not deleted.

If you downgrade from Pro to Free, files you've uploaded remain in your account but become inaccessible until you upgrade again — they are not deleted for 90 days.
