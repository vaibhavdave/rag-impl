---
title: "Adding and Removing Team Seats"
category: admin
---
Org admins can manage seats from Admin Console → Members:

- **Add a seat**: "Invite Member" → enter email → they receive an invite link. New seats are prorated and billed immediately for the remainder of the current cycle.
- **Remove a seat**: "Remove" next to a member — this is immediate; the member loses access right away and their seat's cost is credited toward next cycle's invoice (not refunded for the current cycle).
- **Pending invites**: an invited-but-not-yet-accepted seat still counts against your seat limit and is billed; cancel a pending invite from the same Members page if it's no longer needed.

Only users with the "admin" role can add/remove seats. Regular members can view the team roster but not modify it. If a departed employee still shows as an active seat, removing them here immediately revokes their access — there is no separate offboarding step required.
