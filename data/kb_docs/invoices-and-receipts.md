---
title: "Downloading Invoices and Receipts"
category: billing
---
Individual Pro subscribers can download receipts from Account Settings → Billing → Payment History → each line item has a "Download Receipt" link (PDF).

Team and Enterprise org admins can download consolidated invoices from the Admin Console → Billing → Invoices. Invoices include a line-item breakdown per seat and are generated automatically at each billing cycle.

If your finance team needs invoices addressed to a specific legal entity, tax ID, or billing address different from the account owner's, this must be configured under Admin Console → Billing → Invoice Details before the next cycle — changes don't apply retroactively to already-issued invoices.
