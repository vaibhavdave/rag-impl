---
title: "What Happens When a Payment Fails"
category: billing
---
If your card is declined on your renewal date, your account is not immediately downgraded. You enter a 7-day grace period during which you keep full access to your current plan while we retry the charge (attempts on day 1, 3, and 6).

You'll get an email notification on each failed attempt with a link to update your payment method. If payment still hasn't succeeded after the grace period, your account automatically reverts to the Free plan — no data is deleted, and paid features return as soon as you update your payment method and resubscribe.

Common decline reasons: expired card, insufficient funds, or your bank flagging the charge as unrecognized (contact your bank to allow "NIMBUS AI SUBSCRIPTION" charges). Updating your card does not automatically retry the charge — you may need to manually trigger a retry from Account Settings → Billing.
