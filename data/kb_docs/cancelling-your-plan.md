---
title: "Cancelling Your Subscription"
category: plans
---
You can cancel a Pro, Team, or Enterprise subscription from Account Settings → Billing → Cancel Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle — you keep paid features until then, and you are not charged again after that cycle ends.

Cancelling does not delete your account or your data. Your account reverts to the Free plan automatically. Uploaded files and chat history beyond what Free supports become inaccessible but are retained for 90 days in case you resubscribe.

For Team plans, only an org admin can cancel the subscription; individual members cannot cancel the org's plan from their own account.

There are no cancellation fees. Refunds for partial billing periods are not issued except as described in the Refund Policy.
