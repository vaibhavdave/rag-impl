---
title: "Choosing Between Free and Pro"
category: plans
---
Free is a good fit if you use Nimbus AI occasionally (a handful of conversations a week) and don't need file uploads or long context.

Consider upgrading to Pro if any of these are true:
- You regularly hit the 20 messages/day limit on Free.
- You need to upload documents, spreadsheets, or images for the assistant to analyze.
- You're working with long documents or long-running conversations that exceed Free's smaller context window.
- You need priority access so you're not queued during high-traffic periods.

Pro is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time; cancelling reverts your account to Free at the next billing cycle rather than ending access immediately.
